01 · Problem

Problem

Marketplace sellers and enterprise supply chain teams manage hundreds of SKUs across regional warehouses, where reactive chat tools, alert-count queues, and unguarded pricing or restock edits leave tens of thousands of dollars in margin exposed.

Seller Operations & Supply Chain

Overview

SellerAmelia combines proactive Seller Central monitoring with multi-echelon supply chain planning on serverless AWS infrastructure. Across a 120-SKU multi-warehouse catalog, anomaly_radar.py computes 30-day contribution margin exposure in 0.489 ms (p50), capturing 81.22% of total profit at risk in the top 10 morning items compared with 6.48% when sorting by raw alert count. When an issue surfaces, a Supervisor routes telemetry across four specialist agents, computes coupled demand and lead-time safety stock alongside N-tier Bill-of-Materials (BOM) delay propagation, synthesizes an executive brief with Amazon Bedrock (us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0), and enforces hard margin-floor and Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) checks before committing approved actions.

01 · ALERT FATIGUE

Alert Counts Bury High-Margin Losses

A $9.99 accessory with 46 minor catalog warnings crowds out a single hazmat listing suppression on a $299.00 power station losing $54,644.80 in net margin over two weeks.

02 · COUPLED LEAD-TIME RISK

Static Reorder Rules Miss Vendor Variance

Safety-stock formulas that treat supplier lead times as fixed constants under-buffer when port delays hit, while single-warehouse ordering ignores surplus inventory sitting in sister regional centers.

03 · UNGUARDED REPRICING

Blind Buy Box Chasing Erodes Unit Margin

Automated repricers that match competitor undercuts without deducting referral fees, FBA pick-and-pack fees, storage surcharges, and customer return drag silently push SKUs into net losses.

04 · MESSY ERP FEEDS

Inconsistent 3PL Spreadsheets Corrupt Plans

External SAP, NetSuite, and 3PL CSV exports mix week and day lead times, foreign currencies like EUR, negative cycle-count balances, and TBD placeholders that break naive parsers.

01 · PROACTIVE DOLLAR-IMPACT RADAR seller_amelia/anomaly_radar.py
Instead of sorting notifications by raw event count, the radar evaluates 120 SKUs across four regional fulfillment centers and ranks listing suppressions, Buy Box losses, customer return spikes, 181-to-365-day FBA aged surcharges, and vendor lead-time slips by 30-day profit exposure.
scan_anomalies() runs compute_unit_economics() across all 120 SKUs in 0.489 ms (p50), deducting referral fees (8% to 15%), FBA pick-and-pack fees, monthly storage, and expected return processing drag. Top-10 dollar triage captures 81.22% of $350,390.13 total margin exposure vs. 6.48% under alert-count sorting.
02 · COUPLED (s, S) & N-TIER BOM ENGINE seller_amelia/replenishment_bom.py
Supplier delays amplify demand risk and ripple across shared sub-assemblies. The replenishment optimizer computes coupled safety stock, propagates upstream component slips to finished ASINs, and compares cross-warehouse ground transfers against split purchase orders and air freight.
compute_coupled_ss_policy() evaluates SS = ceil(z * sqrt(E[L]*sigma_D^2 + E[D]^2*sigma_L^2)) using inverse normal quantiles. Across 160 60-day Monte Carlo trials, coupled (s, S) with lateral rebalancing cuts stockout day rate from 6.88% to 0.40% (94.2% relative drop) and reduces mean portfolio cost by 62.5%.
03 · 4-SPECIALIST ORCHESTRATOR & GATE seller_amelia/specialist_agents.py
Resolving a real seller or supply chain exception requires cross-checking catalog rules, FBA fee math, warehouse inventory, and customer defect verbatims together before staging any price edit, removal order, or inventory transfer.
SellerAmeliaControlPlane dispatches CatalogComplianceAgent, FBAEconomicsAgent, InventoryReplenishmentAgent, and ReturnsQualityAgent concurrently via ThreadPoolExecutor, calls Amazon Bedrock (us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0, 880.0 ms p50), checks evaluate_action_guardrail() in 0.002 ms, and appends SHA-256 links to SQLite WAL.
04 · ERP HARMONIZER & LAMBDA ROUTER erp_harmonizer.py · server.py
External manufacturers and sellers upload SAP, NetSuite, and 3PL CSV feeds with varied column names and mixed units. The harmonizer maps headers automatically and quarantines corrupt rows instead of coercing bad numbers to zero.
harmonize_erp_csv() normalizes headers, converts explicit duration units like 3 weeks to 21.0 days in 0.062 ms (p50), quarantines EUR amounts without FX rates, negative stock, and TBD strings with 0 silent coercions, and serves all /api/* routes via lambda_handler().
04 · Trade-Offs

Trade-Offs

Every architectural decision balances calculation accuracy, operational safety, latency, and idle cloud cost.

Design Decisions
01 · DETERMINISTIC MATH VS LLM ARITHMETIC

Exact Unit-Economics & Safety-Stock Math Before Bedrock Synthesis

Asking a language model to estimate FBA referral fees or square-root safety-stock terms risks rounding drift and hallucinated margins. SellerAmelia computes all unit economics, BOM delays, and replenishment costs deterministically first (< 0.5 ms), then passes verified JSON figures to Amazon Bedrock solely for executive synthesis.

02 · HARD MARGIN FLOOR VS UNBOUNDED AUTONOMY

1-Click Co-Sign Never Overrides Net-Margin or MOQ Guardrails

Requiring seller co-sign adds one click before mutating live prices or purchase orders, and hard margin-floor rules prevent aggressive loss-leader pricing unless the floor policy itself is updated. In return, 12 / 12 loss-making price edits and sub-MOQ purchase orders were blocked in 0.002 ms even with co-sign active.

03 · STRICT QUARANTINE VS PERMISSIVE IMPORTS

Quarantining Corrupt ERP Rows Instead of Defaulting to Zero

Quarantining rows with unmapped currencies (EUR 38.50), negative on-hand counts (-18), or text lead times (TBD) holds those SKUs out of automated ordering until fixed. That avoids silent 0 coercions that would trigger false stockout panics or mispriced vendor orders.

04 · SERVERLESS LAMBDA VS ALWAYS-ON CLUSTERS

Zero Idle Cloud Cost vs. Ephemeral Container State

Hosting static assets on Private S3 behind CloudFront OAC and running /api/* on AWS Lambda with SQLite WAL in /tmp keeps idle infrastructure cost at $0.00, with the trade-off that demo mutations reset when cold containers recycle. Production multi-tenant deployments swap the store to DynamoDB or Aurora Serverless.
